Stability functions used:
Euler: R(z) = 1 + z
RK2(midpoint): R(z) = 1 + z + z^2/2
RK4: R(z) = 1 + z + z^2/2 + z^3/6 + z^4/24

On the negative real axis the approximate stability endpoints are:
Euler: -2.000000 (theory: -2)
RK2:   -2.000000 (theory: -2)
RK4:   -2.785250 (theory: about -2.7853)

Explicit RK stability functions are polynomials. As x -> -infinity, |R(x)| -> infinity,
so the entire left half-plane cannot lie inside the stability region. Hence explicit methods cannot be A-stable.
